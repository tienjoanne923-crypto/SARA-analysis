# Third party input notices

The proposed original-code licence, if granted by its rights holders, does not cover third-party datasets, embedded ontology content, pretrained model weights or source-derived labels. The metadata here records provenance and identifies acquisition routes; it does not expand the rights granted by source providers.

CTD inputs retain CTD terms and citations. HPO annotations, Mondo mappings, NCBI/ClinVar/GEO files, consensus-expression components and other composite sources retain their applicable source terms. Current general licences verified for GO/GOA, STRING and HPA do not erase separately applicable rights in incorporated sources. GenCC states CC0 with requested attribution. Model revisions and their historical licence notes are recorded separately from the project code.

The older HPA manifest recorded CC BY-SA4.0. The current official general HPA database statement is CC BY4.0, subject to third-party constraints. The old record is preserved; `hpa_licence_correction.json` records the additive correction and scope.

Source pages and existing manuscript references are identified in `source_access.tsv`. No source licences are replaced with MIT, CC0 or a project-wide data licence by this archive metadata.
