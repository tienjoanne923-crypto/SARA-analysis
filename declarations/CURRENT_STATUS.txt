Availability of data and materials

This study reused data from CTD, HPO, GO/GOA, MeSH, the Human Disease Ontology, Mondo, HGNC, NCBI Gene, STRING v12, MANE, UniProt, the Human Protein Atlas, GTEx v8, GenCC, ClinVar and GEO. The expression cohorts were GSE13355, GSE30999, GSE87466 and GSE75214. Resource versions, preprocessing and evaluation definitions are described in the Supplementary Information. Reused third-party resources remain subject to their original access and licensing terms.

The analysis source code, frozen configurations and development selections, preprocessing scripts, environment specifications and input SHA-256 records are publicly available at https://github.com/tienjoanne923-crypto/SARA-analysis, release v1.0.0-rc2 (https://github.com/tienjoanne923-crypto/SARA-analysis/releases/tag/v1.0.0-rc2). Author-owned original code is provided under the MIT licence. A persistent archive DOI has not yet been assigned. This code and provenance release excludes third-party databases, processed research tables, fitted weights and saved prediction arrays. Its reproducibility documentation identifies the inputs that require restoration or regeneration and the limits of historical raw-data reconstruction.
